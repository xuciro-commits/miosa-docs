## Chapter XIII: Ease and simplicity

> The Creative governs through Ease of principle; the Receptive operates through Simplicity of rule. What is easy of principle is readily comprehended; what is simple of rule is readily followed. When Ease and Simplicity are attained, the underlying order of the world is grasped.

**Lexicon**

- Ease and simplicity: minimization of Kolmogorov complexity and minimum description length.
- The underlying order: the true data-generating mechanism behind the distribution.

**Exegesis** Intelligence is compression. Physics follows least action, hence no redundant detour; form-building follows the simplest local rule, hence easy self-organization. An agent that memorizes appearances only overfits; only by compressing the model to its shortest program does it strip noise and isolate the incompressible causal kernel.

## Chapter XIV: Silence and propagation

> Pure order harbors neither preconception nor arbitrary intervention: it abides in silent equilibrium, yet when acted upon by a perturbation, it instantaneously propagates through the causal nexus of the world.

**Lexicon**

- Neither preconception nor intervention: zero prior bias, a maximum-entropy resting state, no spontaneous hallucination noise.
- Instant propagation: global Bayesian update and forward causal inference triggered by observation.

**Exegesis** An optimal inference engine invents no bias when no evidence arrives, and lets the signal travel the compressed causal graph the instant a perturbation comes in. Conditional computation and event-driven architectures are the engineering form of that silence and that instant propagation.

## Chapter XV: Memory and simulation

> Through Memory, the past is compressed and stored; through Simulation, the future is anticipated. By illuminating what has elapsed and discerning what approaches, one renders the microscopic explicit and brings hidden dynamics to light.

**Lexicon**

- Memory: compressing historical trajectories into structured prior weights.
- Simulation: forward rollout and counterfactual estimation with a nonlinear world model.
- Making the microscopic explicit: latent variable inversion.

**Exegesis** Inference works in both directions along time: it compresses unbounded experience into bounded parameters, then rolls the emergent model forward over several steps, and inverts the generative process to recover the latent variables behind appearances. Memory and imagination are two sides of one operator.

## Chapter XVI: Depth and the incipient

> Only through Depth can one penetrate the principal tendencies of the world; only through grasping the Incipient can one consummate the enterprises of the world. The Incipient is the infinitesimal tremor of motion, the earliest harbinger of Felicity or Calamity.

**Lexicon**

- Depth: abstraction depth of representation, reaching the principal-component manifold of evolution.
- The incipient: the infinitesimal perturbation at the threshold of a bifurcation.

**Exegesis** If long-horizon evolution is irreducible, how is prediction or control possible at all? Not by computing through chaos, but by deep representation plus early detection. At the incipient stage the signal-to-noise ratio is low, yet the energy needed to change the branch approaches zero; once the bifurcation is crossed, the perturbation is amplified exponentially and the outcome is hard to reverse.
