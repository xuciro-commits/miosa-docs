## Chapter IX: Images, configurations, dicta

> Script cannot exhaust speech, nor can speech exhaust meaning. Therefore, perceiving the intricate complexity of the world, the sage establishes Images to fathom meaning in full, institutes Configurations to exhaust truth and semblance, and appends Dicta to consummate speech.

**Lexicon**

- Script, speech, meaning: discrete tokens, one-dimensional language, high-dimensional continuous reality.
- The intricate: noisy, nonlinear, high-dimensional appearance of the world.
- Images: geometric representations in a high-dimensional latent space.
- Configurations: structured state tensors separating true modes from spurious ones.
- Dicta: the decoding map from latent states to action and language.

**Exegesis** Natural language is a lossy low-dimensional projection of reality, and writing is a discrete sampling of language; one-dimensional autoregression cannot exhaust the causal structure of the physical world. A genuine intelligence builds three layers: Images that carry high-dimensional meaning, Configurations that separate signal from noise, and Dicta that emit interpretable judgement and action.

## Chapter X: Exhausting number

> By exhausting the parameters of Number, one determines the Images of the world; by interweaving variables through permutation and transposing their matrices, one traces their transitions to completion, thereby composing the structural fabric of the cosmos.

**Lexicon**

- Number: continuous or discrete parameter space, weight matrices, distributions.
- Interweaving and transposing: multi-head cross-attention, non-local products, dimension permutations.
- The fabric: the internal causal graph the model reconstructs.

**Exegesis** High-dimensional representation is not invented but locked in by optimizing over the parameter space until it is unique; multi-dimensional recombination and high-rank tensor transforms then compose an internal order that corresponds to the objective world.

## Chapter XI: Body, time, transition, direction

> The Image articulates structural invariance; the Configuration articulates the temporal state; the Fluxion articulates local transition; the Dictum points toward the trajectory of evolution. As Fluxions and Images stir within the model, Felicity and Calamity manifest without in the world.

**Lexicon**

- Body: structural invariance.
- Time: the global state at a particular context window.
- Fluxion: the micro-gradient along a single latent dimension.
- Direction: the trajectory vector in state space.

**Exegesis** The division of labour is strict: the Image encodes what a thing is, the Configuration encodes the current context, the Fluxion encodes per-dimension sensitivity, the Dictum encodes the best transition. The agent runs counterfactual rollouts inside its model, and the outcome of that rollout appears outside as convergent gain or dissipative collapse.

## Chapter XII: Isomorphic completeness

> Thus the Formal Simulacrum stands in exact isomorphism with the cosmos, and so can encompass the whole fabric of the Way. Its primitive symbols are minimal, yet the equivalence classes it spans are vast; it bounds the transformations of nature without transgression, and molds the myriad things in all their curvature without omission.

**Lexicon**

- The simulacrum: the formal world model an agent builds.
- Isomorphism: structure-preserving mathematical mapping.
- Minimal primitives and vast classes: tiny axiom sets, unbounded generalization.

**Exegesis** Why can a subjective model predict the objective world? Because the two stand in a structure-preserving mapping. A complete model shows two marks: an extreme compression ratio, and the unity of conservation and precision, never crossing a global boundary, never losing a local detail.
