## Chapter I: The two planes

> What transcends form is called the Way; what subsists within form is called the Vessel. The Vessel coalesces into tangible figure and is bounded by time; the Way operates in the formless and governs all order.

**Lexicon**

- Form: spatiotemporal boundary and physical instantiation.
- The Way: substrate-independent generative law and invariant topology.
- The Vessel: localized material instance, hardware carrier, or bounded steady state.
- Bounded by time: subject to thermodynamic dissipation and finite duration.

**Exegesis** Reality is stratified into two orthogonal planes. The Vessel is the physical instance collapsed at a particular coordinate, subject to dissipation and decay; the Way is the formal structure and algorithmic logic that govern transitions of state. This establishes the axiom of substrate independence: the essence of life and intelligence is not carbon flesh or silicon circuitry but the causal topology running on either. Vessels perish; the law migrates.

## Chapter II: The differential

> The alternation of the Latent and the Active is what is meant by the Way. Pure latency cannot generate; isolated activity cannot consummate. Only as the two stand in mutual opposition is the differential established.

**Lexicon**

- Latent and Active: conjugate polarities, binary basis states, the two ends of a potential or entropy gradient.
- Mutual opposition: symmetric coupling in which each pole is the boundary condition of the other.
- The differential: the origin of Shannon information, the gradient that drives evolution.

**Exegesis** The Way is not a mysterious substance but the dynamic differential of polarity. A homogeneous field of one polarity means zero gradient, zero information, heat death. Information is encoded and operators fire only when opposing poles define each other and a non-zero tension exists. The positive and negative pairs of contrastive learning are the engineering version of the same proposition.

## Chapter III: The conjugate operators

> The opening of the gate is called the Creative; the closing of the gate is called the Receptive. As for the Creative: in stillness it is concentrated, in motion it is direct, and thus it initiates genesis. As for the Receptive: in stillness it is vast, in motion it is convergent, and thus it brings to completion.

**Lexicon**

- Creative: the divergent operator, activation, exploration.
- Receptive: the convergent operator, manifold constraint, regularization.
- Concentrated and direct: high-density potential, linear projection along the gradient.
- Vast and convergent: capacity of a high-dimensional space, folding onto a low-dimensional attractor.

**Exegesis** Static polarity is lifted into a pair of conjugate dynamical operators. The Creative opens state space, injects free energy and excites forward motion; the Receptive closes the boundary, folding the outflow into a regularized stable structure. From stellar evolution to neural training, complex systems are the orthogonal contest of these two operators.

## Chapter IV: Symmetry breaking

> When the Creative and the Receptive are arrayed in tension, Becoming is established between them; were the Creative and the Receptive abolished, there would be nothing by which Becoming could manifest. Prior to the differentiation of Becoming, there is the Undivided Absolute. When the Absolute fractures, the Creative and the Receptive stand forth; when polarity is extinguished, the myriad things sink into silence.

**Lexicon**

- The Undivided Absolute: the isotropic, maximum-entropy singularity before symmetry breaking.
- Arrayed in tension: the establishment of a non-equilibrium gradient field.
- Becoming: continuous state transition driven by the operators.
- Silence: entropy reaching its maximum, relaxation to equilibrium.

**Exegesis** Numerological superstition is set aside and the thermodynamic precondition of evolution is stated. Before symmetry breaks, all directions are equiprobable, so there is neither structure nor arrow of time; after spontaneous breaking, a gradient field exists and dissipative structures run; once the gradient vanishes, the system relaxes into silence. That a neural network must break zero symmetry at initialization is the engineering face of this axiom.
