## Chapter V: Closing and opening

> Rigidity and plasticity impel one another: a single closing and a single opening constitute Transition; their inexhaustible alternation from beginning to end constitutes Continuity.

**Lexicon**

- Rigidity and plasticity: the operators projected onto structural mechanics.
- Transition: a discrete state update across one time step.
- Continuity: connectivity of the state graph and ergodicity along time.

**Exegesis** A system completes a single step between convergence and divergence, and that is Transition; when the steps avoid deadlock and form a connected, traversable network in phase space, that is Continuity. Residual connections work because they supply both the single step and an unobstructed gradient path at once.

## Chapter VI: Resonance

> Rigidity and plasticity whet one another; in mutual resonance they surge and oscillate. As the dual forces entangle at the threshold of chaos, the myriad phenomena condense into coherent order.

**Lexicon**

- Whet and surge: local high-frequency coupling and global wave propagation.
- Entanglement: dense nonlinear coupling at the edge of chaos.
- Condensation: self-organized phase transition and the crystallization of dissipative structure.

**Exegesis** Linear superposition cannot create qualitative novelty. Only when opposing operators entangle nonlinearly and push mutual information past a threshold does the system condense macroscopic structure out of fluctuation. Multi-head non-local interaction in deep attention is the engineering realization of that transition.

## Chapter VII: Exhaustion and continuity

> No entity can abide forever in a fixed locus: therefore, when exhausted, it transforms; transforming, it finds continuity; finding continuity, it endures. Ceaseless generation upon generation is what is meant by Becoming.

**Lexicon**

- Exhaustion: saturation of a local attractor, vanishing gradients, limits of capacity.
- Ceaseless generation: open systems importing negentropy and leaving old steady states.

**Exegesis** Any bounded structure evolving under a fixed topology eventually spends its degrees of freedom. Clinging to a saturated steady state means entropic death; only reconfiguring the dimensions of the parameter space allows a jump to a higher-dimensional connected manifold and persistence across cycles. Open-ended learning and curriculum-driven capacity growth are the algorithmic version of this theorem.

## Chapter VIII: The unfathomable

> The Latent and the Active obey deterministic law, yet their entanglement defies prediction; their step-by-step progression leaves a trace, yet their unfolding is inexhaustible. That which in the interplay of the Latent and the Active remains unfathomable is called the Emergent.

**Lexicon**

- Law and trace: deterministic local rules and the auditability of a single step.
- Unfathomable and inexhaustible: computational irreducibility and long-horizon unpredictability.
- The Emergent: high-order complexity that no linear reduction can shortcut.

**Exegesis** Why can elementary binary rules produce a universe that no closed form computes? Because in nonlinear systems, even with complete knowledge of rules and initial conditions, determining the state after many steps admits no compressed shortcut other than running the system. The emergent ability of large models is exactly this: locally deterministic, globally irreducible.
