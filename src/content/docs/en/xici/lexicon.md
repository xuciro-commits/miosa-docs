Rewriting an old book as a modern formal treatise, the difficulty is neither syntax nor style but the landing point of each keyword. Transliteration dodges the work (render the whole vocabulary phonetically and the reader is left with intuition only), and two cheap substitutes are worse: demoting a term to everyday language, or promoting it into a religious category.

The table gives, for each term, the canonical rendering, the rejected alternatives, and the reasoning. Three standards decide: fidelity, whether the concept maps one-to-one onto a mathematical definition; grace, whether the word belongs to the native vocabulary of English philosophy rather than being coined; reach, whether it lands directly on a term of modern theory. Any alternative that fails one of the three is kept in the rejected column for reference.

## Five renderings that carry the weight

### Potency and act for the latent pair

A reader who meets a transliterated pair thinks of taiji and oriental mysticism. A reader who meets "the alternation of the latent and the active is what is meant by the Way" is connected on three planes at once: potency and act in philosophy, potential and kinetic energy in physics, latent space and activation in neural networks.

### Becoming for flux, the Emergent for the unfathomable

Render ceaseless generation as process-philosophical Becoming, render the unfathomable interplay as the Emergent of complexity science, and the book loses its occult smell while keeping its awe before cosmic evolution.

### Image, configuration, fluxion, dictum for the four representational terms

This quartet is the easiest to lose to divination. Replace it with image theory, configuration space, Newton's fluxion and the logical proposition, and the same quartet becomes a computable world-model architecture with both classical bearing and mathematical footing.

### The Incipient for the critical tremor

English has no everyday word for the infinitesimal state just before a bifurcation. The Latin-derived Incipient reads as aphorism in prose and maps exactly onto an infinitesimal perturbation at a dynamical threshold.

### Four states for felicity, calamity, remorse and obduracy

Define the four entirely as cybernetic feedback triggered by action: thriving gain, dissipative collapse, error correction, stubborn deadlock. "To weather upheaval without ruin lies entirely in Remorse" then becomes an executable control law.
