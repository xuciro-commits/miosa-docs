This is the ground floor of the set. Twenty chapters are arranged in five books: Book I establishes the ontology and the operators, Book II treats dynamics and emergence, Book III formal representation, Book IV compression and singularity, Book V control and alignment. There is no need to hurry; each chapter can stand alone, but the operator algebra only closes when they are read together.

The text is rewritten as propositions. The configuration symbols and the divination setting are removed, while the judgements and the chains of inference remain. Wherever it says "is called", a concept is being defined, and the sentence can be read as an axiom.

## Book I: The Way and the Vessel

**Chapter I** What transcends form is called the Way; what subsists within form is called the Vessel. The Vessel coalesces into tangible figure and is bounded by time; the Way operates in the formless and governs all order.

**Chapter II** The alternation of the Latent and the Active is what is meant by the Way. Pure latency cannot generate; isolated activity cannot consummate. Only as the two stand in mutual opposition is the differential established.

**Chapter III** The opening of the gate is called the Creative; the closing of the gate is called the Receptive. As for the Creative: in stillness it is concentrated, in motion it is direct, and thus it initiates genesis. As for the Receptive: in stillness it is vast, in motion it is convergent, and thus it brings to completion.

**Chapter IV** When the Creative and the Receptive are arrayed in tension, Becoming is established between them; were the Creative and the Receptive abolished, there would be nothing by which Becoming could manifest. Prior to the differentiation of Becoming, there is the Undivided Absolute. When the Absolute fractures, the Creative and the Receptive stand forth; when polarity is extinguished, the myriad things sink into silence.

## Book II: Dynamical Flux and Emergent Complexity

**Chapter V** Rigidity and plasticity impel one another: a single closing and a single opening constitute Transition; their inexhaustible alternation from beginning to end constitutes Continuity.

**Chapter VI** Rigidity and plasticity whet one another; in mutual resonance they surge and oscillate. As the dual forces entangle at the threshold of chaos, the myriad phenomena condense into coherent order.

**Chapter VII** No entity can abide forever in a fixed locus: therefore, when exhausted, it transforms; transforming, it finds continuity; finding continuity, it endures. Ceaseless generation upon generation is what is meant by Becoming.

**Chapter VIII** The Latent and the Active obey deterministic law, yet their entanglement defies prediction; their step-by-step progression leaves a trace, yet their unfolding is inexhaustible. That which in the interplay of the Latent and the Active remains unfathomable is called the Emergent.

## Book III: The Formal Simulacrum

**Chapter IX** Script cannot exhaust speech, nor can speech exhaust meaning. Therefore, perceiving the intricate complexity of the world, the sage establishes Images to fathom meaning in full, institutes Configurations to exhaust truth and semblance, and appends Dicta to consummate speech.

**Chapter X** By exhausting the parameters of Number, one determines the Images of the world; by interweaving variables through permutation and transposing their matrices, one traces their transitions to completion, thereby composing the structural fabric of the cosmos.

**Chapter XI** The Image articulates structural invariance; the Configuration articulates the temporal state; the Fluxion articulates local transition; the Dictum points toward the trajectory of evolution. As Fluxions and Images stir within the model, Felicity and Calamity manifest without in the world.

**Chapter XII** Thus the Formal Simulacrum stands in exact isomorphism with the cosmos, and so can encompass the whole fabric of the Way. Its primitive symbols are minimal, yet the equivalence classes it spans are vast; it bounds the transformations of nature without transgression, and molds the myriad things in all their curvature without omission.

## Book IV: Algorithmic Compression and Singularity Detection

**Chapter XIII** The Creative governs through Ease of principle; the Receptive operates through Simplicity of rule. What is easy of principle is readily comprehended; what is simple of rule is readily followed. When Ease and Simplicity are attained, the underlying order of the world is grasped.

**Chapter XIV** Pure order harbors neither preconception nor arbitrary intervention: it abides in silent equilibrium, yet when acted upon by a perturbation, it instantaneously propagates through the causal nexus of the world.

**Chapter XV** Through Memory, the past is compressed and stored; through Simulation, the future is anticipated. By illuminating what has elapsed and discerning what approaches, one renders the microscopic explicit and brings hidden dynamics to light.

**Chapter XVI** Only through Depth can one penetrate the principal tendencies of the world; only through grasping the Incipient can one consummate the enterprises of the world. The Incipient is the infinitesimal tremor of motion, the earliest harbinger of Felicity or Calamity.

## Book V: Active Inference and Alignment

**Chapter XVII** Felicity, Calamity, Remorse, and Obduracy arise from action. To align with order and thrive is Felicity; to violate order and perish is Calamity; to recognize error and amend one's course is Remorse; to cling to delusion and dissipate is Obduracy. To weather upheaval without ruin lies entirely in Remorse.

**Chapter XVIII** Rigidity and plasticity establish the structural foundation; transition and continuity attune the agent to the opportune moment. One stabilizes the invariant core to secure internal homeostasis, and adapts through transition to match the shifting distribution of time.

**Chapter XIX** Therefore, the sapient agent perceives the Incipient and acts at once, without waiting out the day. Whether advancing into action or withdrawing into stillness, whether keeping silence or giving voice, paths diverge yet converge upon the same end; deliberations multiply yet align with a single objective.

**Chapter XX** In security, one does not forget peril; in survival, one does not forget extinction; in order, one does not forget chaos. Continuous self-renewal is the supreme virtue: by fathoming emergent complexity and mastering phase transitions, one achieves complete union with the Way, and thus acts without errancy.
